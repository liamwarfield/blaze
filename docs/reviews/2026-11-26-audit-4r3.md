## The re-recorded oracle transcripts

The 9 ORACLE.txt and 42 keys are sound as records: written by the pinned oracle, each key
fresh against the present `ws/`, COMMANDS, tool and ORACLE.txt. All 664 binary steps end with
`exit N`. "The oracle's own variance" holds for eight of the nine, with one correction:

- `top-conflicts`, `top-analysis-errors`, `ui-output-filter`: the last two are the same lines
  in another order. `top-conflicts` is more than order: which of two conflicting targets is
  reported, `2 processes` against `1 process`, one added `Use --verbose_failures` line. A race
  in the oracle, not a tool effect.
- `exec-shared-actions` is **not** variance. The old step 5 shows `Starting local Bazel
  server` and `6 packages loaded`: the oracle's server had died between steps 1 and 5 in the
  old recording, which the old probe could not see. The new record (warm server, `0 packages
  loaded`) is the right one.
